ABOUT · 沿革

Why Sumina exists

Most people — even fluent readers of modern Japanese — cannot read kuzushiji, the flowing cursive in which nearly every Edo-period book, letter, and ledger was written. Centuries of literature, science, and ordinary life sit locked behind a hand almost no one is trained to decipher.

Sumina pairs SuminaNet, an in-house character-recognition model inspired by KuroNet — the pioneering system built by Tarin Clanuwat and collaborators — with a human correction step and translation, so a scan becomes a reading, and a reading becomes English. Made for researchers, students, and anyone curious enough to look closely.
